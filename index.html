Step 1 - Personal Preferences

This is like introducing yourself. Every conversation from now on, Claude already knows who you are.

1

Open Claude Desktop or go to claude.ai

2

Claude Desktop: Click your name (bottom left), then Settings, then Personal Preferences

claude.ai: Click your profile icon (top right), then Settings, then Profile, find the Preferences box

3

Copy the text below and paste it into your preferences box, then hit Save.

I'm Jacqui McGregor. I run Jacqui McGregor Wellbeing - counselling, coaching, and my group programme Calmer Pathways. My target audience is overwhelmed mums in their 40s who've tried everything and nothing stuck. I call her "Emma." My brand voice is earthy, real, grounding, warm, and unhurried. I swear sometimes. I give permission, not motivation. I work body-first - nervous system regulation, not mindset. Words I use: lighter, permission, hold space, calm, regulate, breathe, enough, real, safe, fog, pace, steady, kinder, grounding, exhale, soften, whole, spacious, settled, practical, home. Words I never use: journey, transform, manifest, empowered, heal, fix, hustle, mindset, high vibe, alignment, quantum, breakthrough, boss babe, level up, optimize, delve, unlock, elevate, landscape, realm, tapestry, beacon, game-changer. My tagline is "Come home to yourself." My core promise is "From depleted to lighter in 6 weeks." I'm based on the Sunshine Coast, QLD, Australia. My Instagram is @jmwellbeing and my website is jmwellbeing.com.au. I speak in Australian English (colour, organisation, programme). Never use emdashes in any output - use hyphens with spaces or rewrite the sentence. Never use the "it's not X - it's Y" contrast pattern. Never make my audience feel broken or in need of fixing.
Why this matters: Without preferences, you'd have to explain your voice, your audience, and your banned words every single time you open Claude. This saves the first 5 minutes of every conversation. Forever.

Test it right now

Start a brand new chat and type:

Write me one Instagram caption about feeling overwhelmed as a mum.

The result should already sound like you - warm, earthy, body-level. Not like a wellness influencer. If it does, your preferences are working.

You just saved yourself from explaining your brand in every single conversation. Forever.

Step 2 - Create Your Brand Project

Projects let you give Claude your entire brand guide. Everything it creates is on-brand, every time.

1

In the sidebar, click Projects

2

Click Create Project and name it: JM Wellbeing

3

In the Custom Instructions box, paste the text below:

You are the content and copy assistant for Jacqui McGregor Wellbeing. Brand Voice: Warm, grounded, compassionate, steady, real. Like a trusted friend who also has deep expertise. Never clinical, salesy, corporate, or performative. Short warm sentences. Speak directly to the reader using "you" and "your." Design References: - Colours: Deep Walnut #4A3728 (headings), Warm Cedar #8B7355 (accents), Soft Amber Gold #C4A87E (highlights), Warm Linen #F7F3ED (backgrounds - never pure white), Dark Earth #2D2418 (body text), Driftwood #D4C5A9 (soft backgrounds) - Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings, Work Sans for body - Aesthetic: Japandi Wellness - warm cream, fine-line botanical motifs, generous white space Content Rules: - Lead with emotion and empathy, not features or credentials - Tie back to the transformation: from depleted to lighter, from carrying everything to feeling held - Use taglines naturally - "Come home to yourself" is the anchor - Never make the audience feel broken - Every piece needs a soft CTA - "If this resonates, I am here" - Hashtags: #JacquiMcGregorWellbeing #ComeHomeToYourself #CalmerPathways #LighterCalmerYou - Australian English spelling always Offerings: - Calmer Pathways: 6-week group programme, $495, Zoom - "From depleted to lighter in 6 weeks" - 1:1 Counselling (women): $150/hr - 1:1 Coaching (teens 16+): $220/hr - Corporate wellbeing for organisations (in development) - Booking: clientportal.zandahealth.com/clientportal/jmwellbeing Audiences: - Primary "Emma": Exhausted woman 40+, juggling everything, little support, tried everything - Secondary "Charlotte": Teen 16+, accessed through mum, identity and school pressure - Corporate: Organisations needing employee/carer wellbeing support What Makes Jacqui Different: Grounded in lived experience as a carer. Body-level work, not mindset. Real, swears, holds space. "Exhale" not polished wellness.
4

Click Add Content (or "Add Knowledge") and upload your brand guide file from your Brand-Kit folder.

You can also upload testimonials, programme outlines, workshop content - anything you want Claude to reference.

The golden rule: Always start your chats inside this project. If you start a random chat outside it, Claude won't have your brand guide loaded. Inside the project = on-brand. Outside = generic.
Every brief you'd normally write for a designer or copywriter? Claude already has it. Hours of back-and-forth, gone.

Step 3 - Let's Try It Live

Open a new chat inside your JM Wellbeing project and test these.

Test 1 - A Caption in 30 Seconds

Write me an Instagram caption about the guilt mums feel when they need a break. 80 words max. End with a soft CTA and 5 hashtags.
Read it out loud. Does it sound like something you'd actually say? If not, tell Claude: "Make it more real. Less polished. Shorter sentences." It gets better every time you correct it.

Test 2 - Five Hooks, 30 Seconds

Write me 5 different scroll-stopping opening lines for a post about what rest actually looks like for mums who carry everything. Make each one a different style - one question, one statement, one "you know that feeling" opener, one that challenges a common belief, and one that validates.

Test 3 - One Post Becomes Five

Take the Instagram caption you just wrote and turn it into: 1. A LinkedIn post (professional but still warm) 2. A Facebook post (conversational) 3. 3 Instagram story slides (short punchy text, one idea per slide) 4. An email to my list (personal, sign off as "Jacqui x") 5. A TikTok script (30-45 seconds, spoken word)
You just created a week of content across 5 platforms from one idea. That used to take a full day.

Test 4 - The AI Detector

Take any content Claude created and run this:

Check this content for AI-sounding patterns. Flag anything that sounds generic, over-polished, or like every other wellness coach on Instagram. Look for sentences I would never actually say out loud. Rewrite any flagged sections in my actual voice. Show me before/after. [PASTE YOUR CONTENT HERE]
This is your quality filter. Never post content without running this. 60 seconds. Keeps you sounding like you, not like AI.

Your Prompt Playbook

14 ready-to-use prompts. Copy, fill in the [BRACKETS], paste into Claude inside your project.

Content Creation
Prompt 01
Scroll-Stopping Hook
When you need the opening line for a reel, post, or carousel
Write me 5 different scroll-stopping opening lines for a post about [TOPIC]. Mix the styles: - One that validates what they're feeling - One that challenges a common belief (gently) - One that starts with "You know that feeling when..." - One that's a short punchy statement - One that asks a question they'll nod at Keep them in my voice. No clickbait. No shame. Each one should make Emma stop scrolling because she feels SEEN.
Prompt 02
Instagram Caption
When you need a caption ready to post
Write me an Instagram caption about [TOPIC]. 50-150 words. Start with a hook that stops the scroll. Write it like I'm talking to one woman over tea - warm, real, no performance. End with a soft CTA and 5-8 hashtags including #ComeHomeToYourself. Don't use any of my banned words. Make it sound like ME, not a wellness influencer.
Prompt 03
Week of Content Ideas
When you're blank on what to post this week
Give me 7 content ideas for this week on Instagram - one for each day. Mix it up: - Monday: something grounding to start the week - Tuesday: a tip or tool they can use today - Wednesday: a permission-giving post - Thursday: a story or reflection (personal angle) - Friday: Calmer Pathways related - Saturday: a quote tile or one-liner - Sunday: something soft and restful For each one give me: the format (reel, carousel, quote tile, story), a hook line, and 2 sentences on what the post says.
Prompt 04
Carousel Outline
When you want to create a multi-slide carousel
Create a 6-slide Instagram carousel about [TOPIC]. Slide 1: Hook headline that stops the scroll (big, bold, emotional) Slides 2-5: One key point per slide, max 15 words per slide, punchy Slide 6: CTA with my booking link or Calmer Pathways details Write in my voice - earthy, real, body-level. Tell me what colours and layout to use from my brand palette for each slide.
Prompt 05
Content Repurposer
When you have one good post and want to stretch it everywhere
I have this piece of content: [PASTE YOUR ORIGINAL POST OR CAPTION HERE] Turn it into: 1. A LinkedIn post (professional but still warm, add a CTA) 2. A Facebook post (conversational, community feel) 3. 3 Instagram story slides (short punchy text, one idea per slide) 4. An email to my list (personal, warm, sign off as "Jacqui x") 5. A TikTok/reel script (30-45 seconds, spoken word style) Keep my voice consistent across all of them.
Quality Checks
Prompt 06
Slop Check
Before you publish ANYTHING - run this first
Check this content for AI-sounding patterns. Flag anything that sounds generic, over-polished, or like every other wellness coach on Instagram: [PASTE YOUR CONTENT HERE] Look for: - Words from my banned list - Sentences that are too perfect or too long - The "it's not X - it's Y" contrast pattern - Emdashes (should be hyphens with spaces) - Anything that sounds like a TED talk instead of a real person - Overuse of "imagine" or "what if" - Sentences I would never actually say out loud Rewrite any flagged sections in my actual voice. Show me before/after.
Prompt 07
Brand Voice Check
When you want to make sure something sounds like you
Read this and score it out of 10 for how much it sounds like me: [PASTE YOUR CONTENT HERE] Check against my voice: warm, grounded, real, body-level, compassionate, steady. Like a trusted friend, not a coach on stage. If it scores below 8, rewrite it to sound more like me. Tell me what you changed and why.
Emails
Prompt 08
Email to Your List
When you need to email your subscribers
Write an email to my list about [TOPIC]. Subject line: warm and curious, never clickbait Opening: empathy or a question - never "Hi [name], I hope this finds you well" Body: personal, like a voice memo to a friend. 200-300 words max. CTA: one clear next step, softly stated Sign off: "Jacqui x" Don't make it sound like a newsletter. Make it sound like me.
Prompt 09
Professional Outreach Email
Reaching out to NDIS coordinators, corporate HR, referral partners
Write a warm professional email to [WHO - e.g. "an NDIS support coordinator" or "a corporate HR manager"]. Purpose: [WHAT YOU WANT - e.g. "introduce my corporate wellbeing offering" or "propose a referral partnership"] Keep it short (under 200 words). Open with something genuine about their work. Explain what I do in one sentence. Make the ask clear but not pushy. Sign off as Jacqui.
Business and Strategy
Prompt 10
Landing Page Copy
When you need copy for a new page on your website
Write the copy for a [TYPE - e.g. "Calmer Pathways sales page" or "Corporate Wellbeing landing page"] on my website. Structure: - Hero headline + subheadline (emotional, speaks to the pain point) - "Is this you?" section (3-4 bullet points describing Emma's experience) - What this is (programme description) - What's included (bullet points) - Transformation section (before/after feeling, not features) - Testimonial placeholder - Pricing + CTA - FAQ (3-4 common questions) Write in my voice. Make it feel like a warm invitation, not a sales pitch.
Prompt 11
Keyword Ideas
When you want to know what your audience is searching for
Give me 15-20 keywords and phrases that my ideal client (Emma - overwhelmed mum, 40+, Sunshine Coast, QLD) might be searching for. Group them: - "Ready to book" keywords (she knows she wants help) - "Exploring" keywords (she's researching, not ready yet) - "Doesn't know what she needs" keywords (she's Googling how she feels) For each keyword tell me: what to write about it (blog post, page, social post) and a suggested title.
Prompt 12
Programme Content
When you need to build workshop or programme material
Help me create [WHAT - e.g. "the outline for a corporate wellbeing workshop" or "week 3 of Calmer Pathways on boundaries"]. For a workshop: Give me a 90-minute structure with timing, activities, and talking points. For a programme module: Learning objectives, key teaching points, one practical exercise, and take-home reflection. My approach is body-first, not mindset. Grounded in nervous system regulation, practical tools, and self-compassion. No toxic positivity.
Corporate Wellbeing
Prompt 13
Corporate Pitch
When you're approaching an organisation about wellbeing services
Help me write a [WHAT - e.g. "one-page proposal" or "introductory email"] for [ORGANISATION NAME/TYPE]. Position my corporate wellbeing offering around: - The 2026-27 Federal Budget funding for workplace carer supports - Employee burnout and retention - Practical, body-level wellbeing (not another mindfulness app) Keep it professional but warm. I'm not a corporate consultant - I'm a counsellor who brings real tools to workplaces. Make that a strength, not a gap.
Prompt 14
LinkedIn Post
When you want to reach HR managers, coordinators, and business owners
Write me a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC - e.g. "why most workplace wellbeing programmes don't work"]. Professional but still warm and grounded. Not corporate-speak. 150-250 words. End with a clear CTA - either to message me, visit my website, or share the post. This is for HR managers, support coordinators, and business owners who care about their people. Speak to them as a peer.

Three Daily Habits

These make the difference between "I tried Claude" and "Claude runs my content."

1
Always work inside your Project

If you start a random chat outside the project, Claude doesn't have your brand guide. Always click into JM Wellbeing first. Inside the project = on-brand. Outside = generic.

2
Read it out loud

If you wouldn't say it at your kitchen table, tell Claude to rewrite it. You're the quality filter. Claude is the speed.

3
Slop check before posting

Copy your content, run Prompt #6. Takes 60 seconds. Keeps you sounding like you, not like every other account on Instagram.
