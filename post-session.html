Tips That Make It Work

The habits that separate "I tried Claude" from "Claude runs my content."

Speak, don't type
Use your microphone. It's faster and your prompts come out more natural because you're using your actual voice.
Push back
If it doesn't sound like you, say "That's too polished. Make it more real." or "Shorter." Claude gets better every time.
Slop check everything
Before posting, paste it back and say "Check this for AI writing patterns. Flag anything that doesn't sound like me."
Always work in your Project
Start chats inside JM Wellbeing so Claude has your brand guide. Outside the project = generic output.
